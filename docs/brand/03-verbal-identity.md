# Verbal Identity

## Voice

Clear, confident, calm, intelligent, concise, human, specific and encouraging. Write for comprehension before cleverness. Explain the workflow and concrete outcome.

## Naming

Use “Web Content Flow” on first reference. “Content Flow” is acceptable thereafter. Do not lead with WCF, WCF AI or WebContentFlow AI.

## Message hierarchy

1. Category: Content operations platform.
2. Proposition: From one Brief to your entire content system.
3. Distinction: One source of truth travels through every connected channel.
4. Emotional benefit: Clarity, momentum and control.
5. Tagline: Create once. Flow everywhere.

## Preferred language

Build, connect, shape, plan, create, refine, organise, review, reuse, move forward and stay consistent.

Avoid revolutionise, disrupt, dominate, explode, 10x, magic, effortless, unlimited and unsupported superlatives.

## Product boundaries

Say “create website content and structure”, not “publish a complete website”. Say “plan social content”, not “publish automatically”. Say “create video scripts”, not “render videos”. Generated work is a draft requiring human review.
