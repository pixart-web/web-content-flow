# Visual Identity System

## Flow Mark

The primary symbol is one continuous geometric path: one input enters a controlled central structure and exits through three aligned outputs. Its negative space suggests an “F”; the forward silhouette subtly recalls a “W”.

The production implementation is intentionally geometric, reproducible and recognisable at favicon size. It is not a claim that final trademark or legal clearance is complete.

### Explored directions

1. **Flow Mark — selected:** continuous path, central bend, three outputs.
2. **Modular Cascade — retained concept:** stacked cards branching from a common edge; rejected as less distinctive at 16 px.
3. **Context Weave — retained concept:** interlocking context bands; rejected as too intricate for compact use.

### Usage

- Horizontal lockup is the normal first-use identity.
- Symbol-only is allowed for favicon, app icon, watermark and constrained navigation.
- Preserve clear space equal to one path stroke around the mark.
- Minimum digital size: 16 px symbol; 120 px horizontal lockup.
- Never rotate, distort, recolour individual strokes, add glow as the only contrast mechanism, or append “AI”.

## Colour

Marketing canvas `#070A12`, surface `#0D1220`, raised surface `#151C2E`, primary text `#F6F8FC`, secondary text `#AAB3C5`. Flow Violet `#7967FF`, Signal Cyan `#35C8F4`, Pulse Coral `#FF7A8A`, Growth Mint `#45D6A0`.

The signature gradient connects violet, blue, cyan and mint. It identifies flow paths and selected moments; it is not applied to every button or paragraph.

## Shape and composition

Radii are 8, 12, 18, 24 and 28 px. Borders are normally 8% white, reaching 16% on elevated interactive elements. Wide ambient shadows support hierarchy. Product-native cards, paths and structured frames replace stock AI imagery.

## Assets

Authoritative production assets are under `public/brand/` and `public/og/`. The SVG source remains editable and should be subjected to trademark, optical and print-production review before a large public launch.
