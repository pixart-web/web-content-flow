# Social Launch Asset Sources

`src/content/social-asset-templates.ts` defines ten content templates across LinkedIn landscape, Instagram portrait/square, X landscape, YouTube thumbnail and Open Graph dimensions.

Templates cover launch announcement, private beta, workflow, Brand Profile, Website, Social, Calendar, Library, founder message and product principle. They use the Flow Mark, Midnight canvas, Geist typography, one gradient path and an authentic product still.

These are source templates, not published campaign assets. Launch dates, customer proof, prices and wait-list counts are deliberately absent. Final export requires brand review, accurate current status and channel-safe-zone QA.
