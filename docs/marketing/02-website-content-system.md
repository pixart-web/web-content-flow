## Editorial rules

No fake customer proof, metrics, prices, certifications or awards. Capability copy names the exact boundary. Resources without approved articles use a useful editorial empty state. Legal text requires final counsel review before public launch.

## Future CMS boundary

A future content adapter may load typed front matter or CMS records into the same page models. Navigation, components and route contracts should not depend on a vendor schema.
